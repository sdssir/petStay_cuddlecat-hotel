Welcome · Selamat datang

More than a room. A place to play.

Cuddle Cat Hotel is a cat-only hotel in Bandar Laguna Merbok, Sungai Petani. Every guest has a private wooden suite with a resting shelf, their own bowls and litter tray, and time in the Cuddle Playground: a wall of shelves, cubes and rope bridges, tall cat trees, tunnels and a slide on soft grass.

We keep the space quiet, clean and cool, and we check on every cat's eating, playing and resting each day, so you can travel without worrying.

  • Cats onlyNo dogs, no barking. A calm space built around feline comfort.
  • Air-conditioned, with air purifier and water fountainCool, fresh air and running water in the play area.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What should I bring at drop-off?

Your cat's usual food for the length of the stay, their vaccination card, and anything that smells like home, such as a small blanket or toy. Suites have bowls, water and litter trays.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 10am to 6pm. Drop-off and pick-up are arranged by appointment inside those hours, so tell us your preferred slot when you book.

Does my cat need to be vaccinated?

For the safety of every guest we ask that cats are vaccinated and free from fleas. Mention any medical condition in the notes when you book and we will advise.

Can two cats from the same home share a suite?

Tell us when you book. Depending on the cats and the dates we can usually place bonded cats together or in neighbouring suites.

Will my cat get time outside the suite?

Yes. Guests get time in the Cuddle Playground with its wall shelves, cubes, bridges, trees, tunnels and slide. Shy cats are given their own quieter sessions.

Can I get updates while I am away?

Yes. Just ask in the WhatsApp chat and we will send a photo or short video of your cat.

Is a WhatsApp request a confirmed booking?

No. It is a request. We reply to confirm availability, your slot and the price. Your booking is confirmed only when we say so in the chat.

How do I pay?

We confirm the price on WhatsApp before your arrival. Payment is made at the hotel. No payment is taken through this website.
